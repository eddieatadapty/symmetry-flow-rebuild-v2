# Symmetry Growth Audit

**Live → https://eddieatadapty.github.io/symmetry-flow-rebuild-v2/**

Five ideas to turn more Symmetry users into subscribers, each built into a clickable version of Symmetry's own onboarding. Prepared by Adapty.

| # | Idea | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Ask what they’re training for, then say it back | +10–20% CR to purchase |
| 02 | Show the free week on the paywall most people see | +10–20% CR to trial or purchase |
| 03 | Clear the road to the paywall | +10–15% CR to purchase |
| 04 | Give them their rank before the paywall | +5–15% CR to purchase |
| 05 | One honest offer for people who close it | +5–10% ARPU |

Every price, rating and feature comes from Symmetry's App Store listing, website or in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty’s expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across 30,000+ subscription apps, not measured lift for Symmetry. They overlap, so they don’t add up.

Static single page, no build step: `python3 -m http.server`.
